Delete your Śpiewnik account

You can ask us to delete your Śpiewnik account and everything stored with it at any time. This page explains how, and exactly what gets removed.

How to request deletion

Email martin.hollingham+spiewnik@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account".

We confirm the request and remove the account within 30 days — usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials (email, password).
  • Your entire song library — every title, artist, lyric and translation you saved.
  • Your profile, including any DeepL API key you added and your language settings.

Nothing is retained after deletion. There is no separate category of data kept for a longer period; the account and all data tied to it are removed together.

Remove individual songs yourself

If you only want to remove particular songs rather than your whole account, you can delete any song from your library inside the app at any time — open the song and choose to remove it.

Questions

Any questions about deletion or your data: martin.hollingham+spiewnik@gmail.com. See also our privacy policy.
