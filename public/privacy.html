Śpiewnik — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Śpiewnik ("the app") is a personal language-learning tool for reading song lyrics with translations. This policy explains what the app stores and who it is shared with. It is written in plain terms because the app is small and the data it handles is minimal.

What we collect

  • Account email and password. When you create an account you provide an email address and a password. These are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase, and are used only to sign you in. Passwords are stored by Supabase in hashed form; the app never sees your raw password after sign-in.
  • Your library. The songs you add — title, artist, language, the lyrics you paste, and their translations — are stored in your private account so they are there the next time you sign in.
  • An optional translation key. If you add your own DeepL API key, it is stored in your private profile and used only to run your translations.

What we do not collect

The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party cookies. It does not collect your location, contacts, or device identifiers, and it does not build a profile of you.

Your data is private to you

Each account sees only its own library. Isolation is enforced in the database with row-level security, not just in the interface. Nothing you add is shared with other users.

Services the app talks to

To do its job the app sends requests to a few services. These receive only what is needed for the feature you are using, at the moment you use it:

  • Supabase — accounts and database (stores your email and library).
  • DeepL (or Google Cloud Translation) — the text of a line you choose to translate.
  • YouTube and Spotify — a song's title and artist, to find a matching video or track for playback.
  • iTunes Search API — your search text, when you search for a song to add.
  • lyrics.ovh — a song's title and artist, to attempt an automatic lyric lookup.

These services have their own privacy policies and process that data under their own terms.

Pronunciation practice

The practice feature uses your device's built-in speech recognition to score how closely you repeat a line. Audio is processed by your device/browser's speech service and is not recorded or stored by the app.

Deleting your data

You can remove any song from your library at any time. To delete your account and all of its data, email the address below and we will remove it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it.
