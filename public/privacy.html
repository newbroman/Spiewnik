Śpiewnik — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Śpiewnik ("the app") is a personal language-learning tool for reading song lyrics with translations. This policy explains what the app stores and who it is shared with. It is written in plain terms because the app is small and the data it handles is minimal.

What we collect

  • Account email and password. When you create an account you provide an email address and a password. These are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase, and are used only to sign you in. Passwords are stored by Supabase in hashed form; the app never sees your raw password after sign-in.
  • Your library. The songs you add — title, artist, language, the lyrics you paste, and their translations — are stored in your private account so they are there the next time you sign in.
  • An optional translation key. If you add your own DeepL API key, it is stored in your private profile and used only to run your translations.

What we do not collect

The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party cookies. It does not collect your location, contacts, or device identifiers, and it does not build a profile of you.

Your data is private to you

Each account sees only its own library. Isolation is enforced in the database with row-level security, not just in the interface. Nothing you add is shared with other users.

As the person who operates the app, I hold the keys to the database and can therefore see what it contains, including your library, when administering the service — for example to fix a fault, investigate abuse, or respond to a legal requirement. I do not read or use your content for any other purpose, and I never share it or sell it. If you would prefer not to store a song at all, you can remove it at any time.

Services the app talks to

To do its job the app sends requests to a few services. These receive only what is needed for the feature you are using, at the moment you use it:

  • Supabase — accounts and database (stores your email and library).
  • DeepL (or Google Cloud Translation) — the text of a line you choose to translate.
  • OpenAI — used for the optional smart features: a short voice recording when you use pronunciation practice or voice search (transcribed with Whisper), and text for AI song suggestions, emoji illustration, tidying pasted lyrics, and pulling the lyric out of a web page you point it at. These are processed to return a result and are not stored by OpenAI for training.
  • Spotify, iTunes and Deezer — your search text, to find songs to add; and a song's title and artist, to find a matching track for playback.
  • YouTube — a song's title and artist, to find a matching video for playback.
  • lyrics.ovh and public lyrics pages — a song's title and artist for an automatic lyric lookup; and, when you ask the app to find or extract lyrics, it fetches the relevant web page to read the lyric from it.

These services receive only what the feature needs, at the moment you use it, and process that data under their own privacy policies and terms. The app does not send your voice or lyrics to any of them except as described above.

Pronunciation practice and voice search

When you tap to be scored on a line, or search by voice, the app records a short audio clip and sends it to OpenAI's Whisper service to turn it into text. The clip is used only to produce that text and is not stored by the app. These features need an internet connection; if you would rather not send audio, simply don't use voice search or the speaking score.

Deleting your data

You can remove any song from your library at any time. To delete your account and all of its data, email the address below and we will remove it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
